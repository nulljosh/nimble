Privacy Policy

Nimble Answers · Last updated 23 August 2026

Nimble Answers has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, stored or sold. The app is not able to identify you, because it never asks who you are.

What leaves your device

Only the text of the question you type, and only when answering it requires information the app does not already have.

  • Math stays on your device. Arithmetic and related calculations are evaluated locally. Nothing is sent.
  • Factual and definition questions are sent to DuckDuckGo's Instant Answer API and to Wikipedia, and where those cannot answer, to the app's own answering service at nimble-answers.trommatic.workers.dev, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI.

These requests carry your question and nothing else. No identifier, no account, no device fingerprint, no location, no contacts, no history of what you asked before.

What is kept

The answering service keeps no record of your questions or the answers it returns. It holds a short-lived count of requests per network address purely to enforce a rate limit and prevent abuse. That count expires on its own and is never linked to a person.

Your recent searches and your chosen theme are stored on your device only, and are removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia and Cloudflare each receive the question text when a request reaches them, and each handles that request under its own privacy policy. The app shares nothing with them beyond the question itself.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
