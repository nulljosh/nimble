Answers, not ten blue links.

Math and units, offline

Arithmetic, trig, powers, percentages and unit conversion, evaluated on the device. No network needed.

Two models, one sentence

Gemma and Qwen answer in parallel, grounded in live Wikipedia. Numbers are cross-checked against DuckDuckGo. Every answer names its source.

Graphs

Type y = x^2 or plot sin(x) and get the curve, sampled on device.
