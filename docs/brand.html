Voice

Plain and short. Say what it does, then stop.

  • Ask a question. Get one sentence back.
  • It says where the answer came from.
  • No accounts. No telemetry.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use flat fills, one colour per shape.
  • Put black ink on yellow or cream.
  • Set every word in Nimble Sans.
  • Keep Flit and Fen whole.

Don't

  • Add gradients, shadows or glows.
  • Use emojis, in the product or the copy.
  • Use em dashes. A full stop does the job.
  • Reach for purple or teal.
  • Set anything in monospace or a serif.
  • Put words inside the app icon.
